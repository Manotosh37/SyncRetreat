# Indian Visa Guide

Obtaining visa as a remote worker or digital nomad for long term is a little tricky in India. But fret not, we have got you covered. India does not have a separate "Digital Nomad Visa" yet, but you can obtain a visa to visit and work from India. Here at SyncRetreats we will help you with the visa process.

## Your country of origin matters a lot.

If you are from one of the following countries, you can obtain a tourist visa for 1 year on arrival and can work from India as a remote worker or digital nomad:

1. United States of America
2. United Kingdom
3. Australia
4. France

## Step-by-step guide:

- **Gather all required documents**: Prepare your passport, photos, and professional proof.
- **Consult an immigration lawyer**: In India, certain long-term visas must be processed with the assistance of an immigration lawyer.
- **Complete the application**: Work with your lawyer to fill out all required forms accurately.
- **Visa Fees**: The fees typically include $250 for the National Immigration Agency (submitted by your lawyer) and $50 for the Visa Card, paid upon collection.
- **Submission**: Your lawyer will submit the complete application package to the relevant authorities.
- **Processing Time**: The entire process usually takes around 40 days.
- **Approval & Collection**: Once approved, you can proceed to collect your digital nomad visa card.
- **Validity**: The Short-Stay Visa for Remote Workers is usually issued for nine months and can be renewed for another nine months.
