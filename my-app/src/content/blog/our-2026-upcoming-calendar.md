## WHY WE CHOOSE TO START WITH LADAKH

A best place in India where you can be to escape the rains, european heat and many more. Where most of India is raining, ladakh is sunny and dry. A perfect place to escape the monsoons and have a productive work retreat. A place which matches the vibe of Bali but with a tint of Spirituality and Himalayas.

Our Ladakh Retreat is designed to help you disconnect from the distractions of daily life and reconnect with yourself. We believe that true productivity comes from a place of balance, and our retreat is designed to help you find that balance.
